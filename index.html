1. Title of the Application

NourishGuard AI: Intelligent Smart-Pantry Management, Predictive Recipe Generation & Community Food-Rescue Ecosystem.

2. Problem Statement

Over 1/3rd of all food produced is discarded globally. Households lose $1,500–$2,200 yearly due to forgotten expiration dates, poor meal planning, and lack of immediate community donation channels, generating 8-10% of global greenhouse emissions.

3. Proposed Solution

A mobile & web platform providing automated OCR receipt and fridge scanning, generative AI recipes using near-expiry ingredients, and a hyperlocal 30-minute food rescue matching network.

8. Artificial Intelligence Features (Mandatory)

  • Feature 1: Transformer TrOCR + YOLOv8 Freshness Computer Vision for automated grocery receipt & fridge ingestion in < 4 seconds.
  • Feature 2: Generative "Zero-Waste Chef" (LLM + RAG) creating step-by-step custom recipes prioritizing items closest to expiration.
  • Feature 3: Predictive Spoilage & Dynamic Redistribution Matchmaker (Gradient Boosted Trees + Geospatial K-Means).
