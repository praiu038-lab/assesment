Section 02

Problem Statement

What Problem?

Over 1/3rd of all food produced globally is wasted. Over 60% of this waste happens at the household level due to forgotten expiration dates, poor meal planning, and lack of easy food donation channels.

Who Faces It?

Busy working professionals, families, students, and local grocers who buy fresh items in batches but discard them when spoiled, while nearby low-income shelters face constant food shortages.

Why Is It Important?

Food waste generates 8-10% of global greenhouse emissions (potent methane gas in landfills) and costs an average family $1,500–$2,200 annually while hunger remains pervasive in local communities.

Section 03

Proposed Solution

NourishGuard AI transforms how households manage food through a proactive 3-pillar ecosystem:

1. Instant Smart Tracking

Users snap a quick photo of their grocery receipt or fridge shelf. Vision AI & TrOCR parse item names, categorize them, and compute dynamic expiration dates automatically.

2. Zero-Waste AI Chef

An LLM culinary engine detects items entering the "yellow/red" expiry zones and generates tailored, step-by-step recipes utilizing exclusively on-hand ingredients.

3. Hyperlocal Rescue

When food cannot be consumed in time, a 1-tap donation button broadcasts the surplus to verified local shelters, NGOs, and neighbors on a live interactive map for rapid pickup.

Section 05

Objectives of the Application

  • Reduce Household Food Waste by 40%: Provide early warning alerts and proactive consumption reminders before food spoils.
  • Save 15–25% on Monthly Grocery Bills: Prevent accidental over-purchasing and maximize ingredient longevity.
  • Automate Data Ingestion via AI OCR: Ingest 20+ grocery items from a paper receipt or photo in under 4 seconds.
  • Enable 30-Minute Food Redistribution: Connect surplus edible food to local shelters within a 5 km radius seamlessly.
  • Promote Sustainability Literacy: Measure and reward reduced carbon footprint with community impact badges.

Section 08 (Mandatory Requirement)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features

Feature 1: Multi-Modal Receipt & Pantry Vision Scanner

Combines Transformer-based Optical Character Recognition (TrOCR) with YOLOv8 produce object detection models.

What AI is used?

Vision-Language Foundation Models + TrOCR + Convolutional Freshness Classifiers.

Why is it needed?

Eliminates the #1 reason users abandon pantry apps: tedious manual typing of 30 grocery items.

How it helps users?

Snaps a receipt or fridge photo; automatically extracts names, quantities, and auto-computes shelf-life in seconds.

Feature 2: Generative "Zero-Waste Chef" Recipe Engine

Fine-tuned LLM with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) mapped to a verified culinary knowledge graph.

What AI is used?

Specialized Culinary LLM + Vector Similarity Search (Milvus/pgvector).

Why is it needed?

Overcomes "fridge paralysis" when users have 3 random at-risk ingredients and don't know how to cook them.

How it helps users?

Dynamically constructs delicious recipes using at-risk items with exact step-by-step cooking instructions and smart substitutions.

Feature 3: Predictive Spoilage & Dynamic Redistribution Matcher

Predictive Machine Learning (Gradient Boosted Trees) + Geospatial K-Means Cluster Routing.

What AI is used?

Household Consumption Velocity Predictor + Geospatial Routing AI.

Why is it needed?

Predicts if an item will expire before the family finishes it, proactively suggesting charity pickup.

How it helps users?

Matches donors with the closest verified food bank and creates an optimal pickup window with zero wasted travel.
